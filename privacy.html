Privacy & Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how FenceForge collects, uses, protects, and manages information when you visit our website, contact us, or request fencing services.

Introduction

Welcome to FenceForge. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling the information you share with us responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you visit our website or use our services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact FenceForge, request a free measurement, ask for a quotation, or communicate with us through the website.

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Property or project information.
  • Preferred fencing material or service.
  • Information included in messages or enquiries.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through the FenceForge website may be used to understand your fencing requirements, respond to enquiries, and provide information about relevant services.

  • Respond to contact requests and questions.
  • Arrange or discuss free property measurements.
  • Understand project and fencing requirements.
  • Provide requested service or product information.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.

Free Measurement Requests

When you submit a free measurement request, FenceForge may require basic contact and project information so that your request can be reviewed and the appropriate next steps can be discussed with you.

Please provide only information that is reasonably necessary for your fencing enquiry.

Information Sharing

FenceForge does not intend to sell personal information submitted through this website. Information may be handled by services or systems required to operate the website, communicate with visitors, or process service enquiries.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

Data Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information submitted through the website from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting information over the internet or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Cookies & Browser Storage

FenceForge may use browser-based technologies where they are required for website functionality. For example, browser storage may be used to remember interface preferences such as light or dark mode and left-to-right or right-to-left display direction.

If additional analytics or tracking technologies are added to the website in the future, this policy should be updated to describe their use.

Third-Party Services

Some website functionality may rely on third-party technologies or service providers. Those services may operate under their own privacy practices and policies.

FenceForge is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites that may be linked from this website.

Your Choices

You may choose not to provide personal information through the website. However, certain details may be necessary for FenceForge to respond to your enquiry or process a free measurement request.

You may contact FenceForge if you have questions about information you previously submitted through the website.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

FenceForge may update this Privacy Policy when website functionality, services, or information-handling practices change.

Updated versions of this policy will be published on this page with the appropriate revision information.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about information submitted through the FenceForge website, please contact us through our Contact page.
