Pricing Factors

What Determines Your Fence Cost?

No two properties require exactly the same fence. The final estimate is shaped by the material you choose, the size and layout of the boundary, installation conditions, and the features included in the project.

A site measurement helps us estimate these factors accurately.

Material Selection

What Your Fence Is Made From

Wood, vinyl, chain-link, and wrought iron each have different material, finishing, maintenance, and installation requirements.

How Much Boundary You Need

How Your Fence Is Configured

Taller fences, privacy configurations, decorative details, custom spacing, and specialized designs can affect material quantities and installation requirements.

Where Your Property Needs Entry

Varies

What’s Included

More Than Materials. A Complete Installation.

A FenceForge project covers the important steps from initial measurement through final inspection, so your estimate reflects the complete installation rather than materials alone.

01 — 07

From the First Measurement to the Final Check.

We look at the complete project before installation begins. That means planning the fence line, understanding access, selecting the right materials, installing securely, and checking the finished work. We also consider gate placement, property layout, installation requirements, and site-specific details to help ensure every part of the project is properly planned and carried through from start to finish.

Clear project scope before work begins, with the installation requirements reviewed around your actual property, including access points, fence layout, gate requirements, material selection, site conditions, and the details needed to keep the installation organized from measurement through completion.

Discuss Your Project
01

Property Measurement

We review the proposed fence line, dimensions, access points, layout, and property requirements.

02

Fence Planning

Material, height, layout, privacy, security, and gate requirements are planned around the property.

03

Selected Fence Materials

The required fencing, posts, rails, panels, and related components are based on your selected solution.

04

Professional Installation

Posts, sections, alignment, spacing, and structural components are installed according to the planned fence line.

05

Gates & Hardware

Where included in the project, gates, hinges, latches, and required access hardware are fitted and checked.

06

Work Area Cleanup

Installation debris and normal project waste are cleared from the immediate work area after installation.

07

Final Inspection

The completed fence is reviewed for alignment, installation details, gates, hardware, and overall finish.
